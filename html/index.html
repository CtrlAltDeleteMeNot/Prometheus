Screener About

Prometheus is a crypto market screener built for traders who focus on USDC trading pairs across multiple exchanges.

Instead of manually scanning markets, Prometheus aggregates and filters real-time data so you can quickly identify liquidity, volatility, and emerging trading opportunities.

The goal is simple: reduce noise and surface the signals that matter most to active traders.

If you're interested in custom analytics, automation, or a tailored version of the screener, feel free to reach out via LinkedIn .
